About IEYI

The International Exhibition for Young Inventors (IEYI) was first established in Tokyo in 2004 and has grown into a distinguished international platform for youth innovation and cross-cultural exchange, now encompassing nearly 40 member countries and regions worldwide. IEYI 2026 brings together young inventors and artists in Beijing to showcase creativity, share ideas, and forge lasting international friendships.

Create

Fostering core competencies in critical thinking and problem-solving through hands-on invention.

Cooperate

Building bridges between young minds from different cultures through international exchange.

Contribute

Encouraging innovations that serve society and contribute to a sustainable future.

Artwork

The Artwork category invites participants to express their scientific imagination through visual arts. This category focuses on the theme "Science of Tomorrow" — use your imagination to depict future scenarios of new science and technology.

Requirements:

Artwork must be hand-drawn on B4 drawing paper (38 cm × 27 cm) in 2D form. Any drawing tools are accepted (paint, crayons, markers, etc.). Digital art is NOT accepted. Include a title and a brief description (max 300 words). AI must not be used in the conception or creation of the entry.
